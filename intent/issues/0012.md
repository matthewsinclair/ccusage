## Decision

hv's decision 18 of 2026-11-17 (on hv's board, relayed by vc): in the next release, r in the Herdr panel forces a fetch, as the dashboard's r does. ic builds it, in one worktree and one Desk cargo slot.

## Symptom

The panel asks ccusage on its timer, which is never shorter than the usage cache's lifetime (five minutes), and it asks from the cache. Its one command, `ccusage.refresh`, which a confirmed Use sends, also asks from the cache. So the panel has no way to show fresh usage before the cache's lifetime runs out. The dashboard's r fetches every account at once.

## Fix outline

1. `ccusage.refresh`, the command a key sends, keeps its name and now forces a fetch. The panel runs `ccusage which --json --refresh`, which fetches every account at once, but not one that is in a rate-limit cooldown, as the dashboard's r does. Then it runs `predict --json` from the fresh cache. hv binds the key in keys.json: `"mod:ccusage": {"r": "ccusage.refresh"}`. ccusage never writes keys.json. The key reaches the panel while the panel holds the keyboard, which a click gives it.
2. While the fetch runs, the panel says "Refreshing accounts". A press while one runs starts no other fetch, and the panel says "Refresh already in progress", the dashboard's words. Every copy of the mod shares one forced fetch, so a press that reaches two shown panels fetches once.
3. The five-minute timer stays as it is, and asks from the cache. The reload after a Use from a card's dialog keeps an unforced path of its own, a command named `ccusage.reload`, because a switch needs no fetch.
4. Docs: README's refresh policy says that a key press forces a fetch (the dashboard's r and the panel's), so the five-minute bound and the count per hour describe the timers. README's Herdr section and RECIPE.md show the keys.json line for r as hv's to add. The CHANGELOG gets a line under 0.1.11.

## Acceptance

1. With hv's keys.json line, r in the panel runs `which --json --refresh`, then `predict --json`, and the panel shows the answer. Its "as of" moves when the fetch ends, well before the timer's next tick.
2. While the fetch runs, the panel says it is refreshing, and a second press starts no other fetch.
3. The timer, and the reload after a Use, still ask from the cache.
4. `herdr-app check` passes on the installed mod, and README, RECIPE.md and the CHANGELOG say how r works and that the keys.json line is hv's.

## Proof

The mod has no harness that can run it outside Herdr, so nothing here can go red first. `herdr-app check` loads the mod and renders it once: that catches a broken mod, but not a wrong refresh. The proof is `herdr-app check` on the built mod, plus hv's look.
